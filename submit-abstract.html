Submit Your Talk Details

This self-service form replaces the former email submission process. After you submit, your name, affiliation, title, and abstract will be published on the Upcoming Seminars page automatically.

You will be asked to sign in to a free GitHub account. This identifies the submitter, lets you edit your own talk later, and protects the schedule from anonymous spam.

What happens next?

  1. Complete the short form and select Submit new issue on GitHub.
  2. An automatic check confirms that the date is valid and still available.
  3. Your seminar information appears on this website, usually within one minute.

GitHub's “Add a title” is only a pre-filled booking label, not your presentation title. Enter the presentation title in the Talk title field. To revise your information, edit the description of the same GitHub submission linked from your seminar card; do not open a second issue. To withdraw, close that submission.
