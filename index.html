About the seminar

The IMFP-IGP Seminar welcomes talks from a broad range of areas in mathematics and related fields. Talks typically last one hour.

In-person seminars take place on Thursdays from 2:30 PM to 3:30 PM at the Institute for Mathematics and Fundamental Physics (IMFP) in Shanghai. Online participation is also available via Tencent Meeting.
